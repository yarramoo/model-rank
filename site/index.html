    Where the numbers come from

      Parameter counts are read from each model's published weights where possible, and otherwise from the maker's stated or nominal size. Active counts for mixture-of-experts models are the maker's figure, or an estimate from the model's configuration when the maker gives none. Estimates and third-party figures are marked with a tilde; hover a size to see where it came from. Scores belong to the benchmark publishers and are shown with attribution. A benchmark score is one measurement on one task set, so read a ranking as a shortlist and test the top few on your own work.
